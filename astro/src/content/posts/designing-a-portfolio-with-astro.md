# Designing a Portfolio with Astro

I wanted my portfolio to be simple to update but still feel custom. Astro content collections were the perfect middle ground: a clean schema for metadata and markdown for the long form.

## What I focused on

- A consistent card system for projects and posts.
- Fast page loads by keeping client-side scripts minimal.
- A layout that feels crafted without being heavy.

## Why Astro works for this

Astro lets you mix components and markdown without the overhead of a full SPA. It is ideal for content-heavy pages and lets you scale the site without rewriting the fundamentals.
